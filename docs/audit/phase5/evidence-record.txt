== 3. STATUS LABELS ==
S = settled evidence on record: the evidence for the candidate classification is complete. Two different reviews exist and this distinction matters: evidence review (is the evidence complete) and the adoption review (the required per-item review before any candidate status takes effect). S speaks only to evidence. Where a settled row says 'required review pending', that is the adoption review, which applies to every row - not missing evidence.
P = provisional: evidence in hand (a pinned run), not yet through final evidence review; nothing P-tier feeds the totals as final.
O = open: a review call, conditional owner item, or contingent route remains.
Variants: S-cand = settled evidence base with the classification still candidate pending required review; S-doc = documented non-work disposition; P/S = provisional mechanism closure over a settled historical base.
No P or O row is a final classification.
