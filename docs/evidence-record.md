## Platform facts

Each outside fact carries its source, the date checked, and a status of Verified or Unproven. Only Verified facts support a requirement. E2 stays Unproven. Keep each fact's date and limits when using it.

- **E1 Pages limits and terms.** Verified October 21, 2026. On GitHub Free, Pages requires a public repo. Published sites are limited to 1 GB, with a soft 100 GB a month, and a soft 10 builds an hour (not applied to custom Actions workflows). The terms say Pages must not run a business, e-commerce, or commercial software as a service, and should not carry sensitive transactions such as passwords or credit card numbers. No legal judgment is made. Sources: [limits](https://docs.github.com/en/pages/getting-started-with-github-pages/github-pages-limits), [creating a site](https://docs.github.com/en/pages/getting-started-with-github-pages/creating-a-github-pages-site).
- **E2 Pages headers observed.** Unproven. Date checked: not recorded. No source, date, or response is recorded for the earlier sample of missing frame, content security, and HSTS headers. It is not a platform claim. A fresh dated sample is recorded as E9.
- **E3 Worker static-file headers.** Verified October 21, 2026. The `_headers` file applies to static asset responses only. Custom headers defined in `_headers` are not applied to responses generated by Worker code, even when the request URL matches a rule; those responses send their own headers. Source: [Cloudflare headers](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers/static-assets/headers/).
- **E4 Render free plan.** Verified October 21, 2026. A free web service spins down after 15 minutes without inbound traffic, including HTTP requests and WebSocket messages, and spinning back up on the next request takes about one minute. Source: [Render free plan](https://render.com/docs/free).
- **E5 Cloudflare free plan.** Verified October 21, 2026. Request caps only: Workers allow 100,000 requests a day, and Durable Objects allow 100,000 requests a day, both resetting at 00:00 UTC. CPU and duration limits are on the linked limits page. Sources: [Workers limits](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers/platform/limits/), [Durable Objects pricing](https://developers.cloudflare.com/durable-objects/platform/pricing/).
- **E6 LiveKit free plan.** Verified October 21, 2026. Monthly allowances on the free Build plan: 5,000 WebRTC participant minutes and 50 GB of downstream data transfer. On the free Build plan the included allowance is a hard cap: after it is exceeded, new requests fail rather than incurring charges. Allowances reset on the first day of each calendar month and are shared across all of a user's free projects. Source: [LiveKit quotas](https://docs.livekit.io/home/cloud/quotas-and-limits/).
- **E7 Resend free plan.** Verified October 21, 2026. 100 emails a day, with 30-day data retention. Source: [Resend pricing](https://resend.com/pricing).
- **E8 Earlier source observations.** Unproven. Read at commit c8c0010, not tested. Camera and microphone are requested on page load. Camera off swaps the sent video track: a canvas photo track replaces the camera track on every sender. Mute disables the audio track. End reconnects the socket. No step that stops the tracks was found. The server accepts any non-empty room name and id. These claims describe only the pinned commit. Source: repository at main `c8c0010`, read October 21, 2026.
- **E9 Pages headers sample.** Verified October 21, 2026. A HEAD request returned server GitHub.com and access-control-allow-origin *, and no X-Frame-Options, Content-Security-Policy, or Strict-Transport-Security. One sample is not proof for every response. Source: HEAD request to https://soliskit.github.io/ on October 21, 2026.
